Who We Are

Professional. Discreet. Consistent.

Kilo Vision Security LLC is a New Jersey–based security company providing armed and unarmed services for nightlife and high-traffic environments. We don’t rely on intimidation. We rely on experience, situational awareness, and controlled response.

Kilo Vision is owner-operated. The same person you speak with oversees staffing, planning, and execution for each job — so you’re not getting random guards who don’t know your venue.

Our Approach

Security should never kill the vibe.
